## 7. Carry-over context (so nothing gets re-litigated)

- Tokyo capped at 8 on purpose.
- Schedule A chosen over B because mid-September is peak Kamikōchi (worth an extra Alps night) and B's 5th Kyoto day, while lovely, wasn't worth trading the Alps day for.
- Hakone dropped because Sep 19 (its original slot) is Silver Week's worst onsen-crowd day; the onsen experience moved to Hokkaido instead.
- Early-Sapporo-on-the-7th requirement is why there's no front-loaded Fuji/Hakone overnight (you must sleep in Tokyo on the 6th for the AM flight). The Sep 7 flight is ANA NH59, HND 10:00 → CTS 11:35 — Tokyo front hotel needs Haneda-line access.
- Arrival is **Narita** (not Haneda) — Sep 3 transfer is Skyliner / N'EX ~60–90 min into central Tokyo.
- Kitchen-supply trail is a deliberate theme: **Kappabashi** (Tokyo) → **Aritsugu** (Kyoto, Nishiki Market) → **Doguyasuji** (Osaka).
