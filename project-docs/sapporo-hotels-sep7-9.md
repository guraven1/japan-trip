# Sapporo Hotel Options — Sep 7–9 leg (2 nights: in Mon Sep 7, out Wed Sep 9)

Trip: Rachel & Gur, Japan, Sep 3–23 2026. This is the Sapporo leg.

## Selection logic
Early September is shoulder season in Sapporo — mild days (~20–25°C), no snow-festival or summer peak, so rates are moderate and availability decent (but book ~now, 7 weeks out, since the best-value rooms and the top splurge place tighten). All picks are walkable to a subway station and to Odori/Susukino dining. Spread across budgets, weighted toward couple-friendly features: hot-spring/onsen baths, good doubles, views. Prices are approximate per night for a double room; confirm live for exact dates.

## The five options (ascending price)

### 1. Dormy Inn Premium Sapporo — best value + onsen ~¥18,000–26,000 (≈$120–175)/night
Location: Tanukikoji shopping street, ~5–6 min walk to Susukino & Odori. Rating 4.3 (#10/173).
- Pros: Top-floor natural hot-spring onsen + sauna; free late-night ramen ("yonaki soba"); superb seafood breakfast; central covered-arcade location (great in rain); excellent value.
- Cons: Rooms are small; women's onsen smaller than men's; mechanical tower parking.

### 2. Cross Hotel Sapporo — best all-rounder / design ~¥20,000–29,000 (≈$135–195)/night
Location: midway between Sapporo Station (~5 min) and Odori. Rating 4.3 (#9/173).
- Pros: Stylish design; 18th-floor bath with city views; unbeatable central position for a couple; English-speaking staff; good Hokkaido breakfast.
- Cons: Standard rooms compact; breakfast queues 7:30–9:00; some rooms run warm.

### 3. Sapporo Stream Hotel — brand-new, in the heart of Susukino ~¥22,000–33,000 (≈$150–220)/night
Location: atop the Cocono Susukino complex, direct subway + mall access. Opened 2024, Tokyu-operated, 4-star. Rating 4.1 (#79/173, few reviews yet).
- Pros: Newest option, very clean/modern; onsen, 18F spa/lounge, rooftop terrace; steps from Susukino nightlife, ramen, dining; direct subway.
- Cons: Rooms compact and a bit dark; premium charges for some amenities; Susukino is a red-light/entertainment district (lively, can feel seedier late).

### 4. Onsen Ryokan Yuen Sapporo — romantic onsen-ryokan ~¥30,000–42,000 (≈$200–280)/night
Location: Chuo-ku, ~5 min to Odori, 12–15 min to Station. Rating 3.7 (#124/173).
- Pros: Modern ryokan feel — tatami + low beds; indoor/outdoor hot-spring baths + sauna; excellent traditional breakfast; the most distinctly "Japanese onsen" romantic vibe.
- Cons: Rooms small with tight layouts; onsen gets crowded evenings; lower overall rating and one review flagged unwelcoming service toward foreign guests — worth weighing.

### 5. JR Tower Hotel Nikko Sapporo — splurge, views, ultimate convenience ~¥32,000–48,000 (≈$215–320)/night
Location: directly on top of Sapporo Station. Rating 4.5 (#4/173).
- Pros: Zero-minute station access (perfect for the day you arrive/leave and for airport/Otaru day trips); 22F natural hot-spring sky spa; 35F restaurant with panoramic breakfast; consistently top-rated staff and views.
- Cons: Priciest here; standard rooms can feel compact and décor a touch dated; station area is quiet after dark.

## Quick recommendation
- Best balance for a couple: **Cross Hotel Sapporo**.
- Splurge / views / easiest logistics: **JR Tower Nikko**.
- Value without giving up onsen: **Dormy Inn Premium**.
- Most romantic onsen vibe (with caveats): **Yuen**.
- Newest + want to be in the middle of Susukino dining: **Sapporo Stream**.
