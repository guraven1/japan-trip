## Suggested day-by-day skeleton

**Sep 7 — Sapporo arrival (easy)**
Land 11:35 → JR Rapid to Sapporo (~37 min) → check in, group lunch. Afternoon (jet-lag friendly): Ōdōri Park + Sapporo TV Tower, the Red-Brick former Government Building, Nijō Market. Evening in Susukino: miso ramen (Ramen Yokochō), soup curry, or jingisukan; a Nikka whisky bar. Optional night cap: **Mt. Moiwa ropeway night view**.

**Sep 8 — Shakotan coast drive (rental car)** ⭐
Pick up the car in Sapporo (~8:30 start keeps it relaxed). **Otaru** (~45 min) — canal + quick seafood/LeTAO → **Yoichi** (~25 min) — **Nikka Whisky** distillery tour & tasting → **Shakotan Peninsula / Cape Kamui** (~1 hr) — the ~40-min cliff walk over the turquoise **"Shakotan Blue"** sea (Shimamui Coast viewpoint too) + uni/seafood lunch. Drive back to Sapporo (~2 hrs) for dinner. *(Tolls ~¥1,400 to Otaru; coastal roads free.)*

**Sep 9 — Onsen ryokan**
Check out → **drive to Noboribetsu** (~1h40 via expressway; optional scenic stop at Lake Shikotsu or a Lake Tōya viewpoint). Walk **Jigokudani "Hell Valley"** + Ōyunuma. ⭐ Check into the ryokan: private/outdoor soak, yukata, kaiseki farewell dinner with the group.

**Sep 10 — Fly south**
Last soak + onsen breakfast → **drive to New Chitose (~50 min), drop the car** → fly to Osaka Itami → Kyoto.

## Onsen ryokan — Sep 9 (all four, 2 rooms, SPLURGE)

**Noboribetsu** — classic hot-spring town, dramatic Hell Valley, **~1 hr from the airport** (easy Sep 10). *Splurge picks — all need vegan+veg confirmation (see ⚠):*
- **Takinoya** — top-tier luxury ryokan; guests specifically praise its *beautiful special vegetarian kaiseki*, so it's the most promising for Rachel + Tamar. Not on Booking — **enquire direct**. Premium.
- **Ryotei Hanayura** — 8.9. Refined ryotei, **kaiseki + breakfast included**, rooms with a **private open-air bath**. ~**$519**/couple.
- **Bourou Noguchi** (adults-only) — 8.8, luxury, private baths, ~**$551**/couple.
- *(Iconic-baths value alternative: Dai-ichi Takimotokan — 8.9 from 3,578 reviews, huge multi-spring bathhouse, ~$240.)*

**Lake Tōya** (if you switch, ~2 hrs from the airport): **Hotel Premium Lake Toya** (9.3, in-room cypress onsen bath + lake view, ~$348) or **Lake Toya Tsuruga Resort Hikarinouta** (9.4, dinner+breakfast, ~$546).

> ⚠ **Dietary — do this BEFORE booking.** Email the ryokan **with the reservation**: **1 vegetarian (Rachel) + 1 vegan (Tamar)**; ask for **kombu-only dashi (no katsuobushi)** and **no dairy/egg** for Tamar; get **written confirmation**; **~2 weeks' notice minimum**. If a ryokan can't confirm, pick one that can.

**My read:** **Noboribetsu**. For a splurge that can handle vegan+vegetarian, **Takinoya** is the front-runner (proven special veg meals), **Ryotei Hanayura** the refined backup. Confirm diets in writing, then book — group rooms sell out.

## Transportation

- **Rental car — YES, Sep 8–10.** Pick up in Sapporo the morning of Sep 8 (Shakotan day), keep it for the Sep 9 drive to the ryokan and the Sep 10 run to New Chitose (drop there). Car-free for the Sep 7 arrival → only one night of city parking. **Book early** (Toyota / Nippon / ORIX / Times, or via ToCoo!/Klook) for these Silver-Week-adjacent dates; note the one-way drop at the airport.
- ⚠ **Driving permit:** each driver needs an **International Driving Permit** (1949 Geneva Convention, **booklet** form, issued in Israel within the last 12 months), carried with the **home licence + passport**. Arrange **before flying** — it cannot be obtained in Japan.
- **Sep 7 (car-free):** New Chitose → Sapporo, JR Rapid Airport ~37 min, ~¥1,150. Around the city: subway + walkable Ōdōri/Susukino; mobile Suica/ICOCA works in Hokkaido.
- **Driving notes:** Otaru expressway toll ~¥1,400; Shakotan coastal roads free; Sapporo→Noboribetsu ~1h40; Noboribetsu→New Chitose ~50 min. Drive on the left; winter tires irrelevant in Sept.
- **Luggage:** the car handles it; just an overnight bag out for the ryokan room.
