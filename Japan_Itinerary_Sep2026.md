## Locked logistics — do these early

- **Arrival:** Sep 3 (Thu), land **Narita (NRT) 13:20** on El Al LY91 (Premium, PNR YJ5PON). Skyliner or N'EX into central Tokyo, ~60–90 min. Day 1 is a soft landing — don't over-plan it.
- **Early Sapporo on the 7th:** Sleep in Tokyo on the **night of the 6th** near a Haneda line (Keikyū line, or central). **Sep 7 flight booked: ANA NH59, Haneda T2 10:00 → New Chitose 11:35 (ref FMSXWA).** New Chitose → Sapporo is ~40 min by Rapid Airport train — in central Sapporo by late morning to meet the group for lunch.
- **Hokkaido onsen night (Sep 9):** Book an **onsen ryokan at Noboribetsu or Lake Tōya** — this is your ryokan-and-private-soak experience, plus a kaiseki dinner as the group's farewell. Both sit between Sapporo and the airport, so you don't backtrack.
- **Hokkaido → Kyoto (Sep 10):** From the onsen area it's a short hop to **New Chitose**; fly **CTS → Osaka Itami (ITM)** (~2h), then Itami → Kyoto (~1h). Itami beats Kansai (KIX) for Kyoto.
- **Silver Week (Sep 19–23):** A rare 5-day national holiday — domestic travel peaks, Tokyo hotels and shinkansen seats sell out months ahead. **Book the late-Sept Tokyo hotel and your Takayama→Tokyo train seats now.** Departure (the 23rd) is itself a holiday — pad airport-train time.
- **Rail:** Skip the full JR Pass; individual tickets are cheaper here. Get a **mobile Suica/ICOCA** for local transit.
- **Luggage forwarding (takkyūbin):** Use it for the Takayama leg so you're not hauling suitcases on the Hida train.
- **Key transfers to reserve:**
  - Osaka → Takayama: Shin-Osaka → Nagoya (shinkansen ~50m) → **Hida** ltd. express Nagoya → Takayama (~2.5h). Sit on the **right** leaving Nagoya for river views.
  - Takayama → Tokyo (the 19th): Hida → Nagoya (~2.5h) → shinkansen → Tokyo (~1.5h). Start early; reserve (Silver Week).

# DAY-BY-DAY

## Shared core — Sep 3–10 (identical in both schedules)

### Sep 3 (Thu) — Tokyo, arrival
- Land 13:20, drop bags. Stay west-side (Shinjuku/Shibuya) or central for Haneda access on the 7th.
- Easy evening: **Shinjuku** — Omoide Yokochō and Golden Gai (tiny bars), or your neighborhood izakaya. Don't fight the jet lag.

### Sep 4 (Fri) — Old Tokyo + kitchen heaven
- **Morning:** Asakusa — **Sensō-ji** + Nakamise street early, before crowds.
- **Late morning:** Walk 10 min to **Kappabashi "Kitchen Town"** — the cook's pilgrimage. Knives (Kamata Hakensha, Tsubaya), ceramics, cast iron, copper, molds, plastic food samples. 1.5–2h.
- **Afternoon:** Cross to **teamLab Planets (Toyosu)** — timed ticket in advance.
- **Evening:** Yakitori under the tracks at **Yūrakuchō**, or sushi.

### Sep 5 (Sat) — Harajuku → Shibuya, gardens + vintage
- **Morning:** **Meiji Jingū** + Yoyogi Park. Coffee at **Fuglen Tokyo** (Tomigaya) by the park.
- **Midday:** **Nezu Museum** garden (Omotesandō) — hidden, beautiful, with a café. Then **Omotesandō / Cat Street** + **Koffee Mameya** for a bean tasting.
- **Afternoon:** **Shibuya** — Scramble Crossing, **Shibuya Sky** at sunset (book ahead).
- *Optional swap:* if the forecast is clear, make today a **Fuji day trip** (Hakone loop or Kawaguchiko + Chūreitō Pagoda, ~2h each way) — only worth it on a clear day.

### Sep 6 (Sun) — Your pick + position for the flight
- Flex day. Strong options: **Shimokitazawa** (vintage paradise — Toyo Department Store, New York Joe, Flamingo, Stickout; cafés like Bear Pond Espresso), or **Kiyosumi-Shirakawa** (Tokyo's coffee district — Blue Bottle Japan flagship + Koffee Mameya Kakeru).
- **Evening:** Pack, sleep near a Haneda line. Early night.

### Sep 7 (Mon) — Fly to Hokkaido
- Early Haneda → New Chitose. Into **Sapporo** by late morning. Meet the group. *(Sapporo, night 1)*
- **Afternoon:** **Nijō Market** (kaisendon — uni, crab, scallops, ikura) or **Sapporo Beer Garden** for jingisukan + beer.
- **Evening:** **Susukino** — Ramen Yokochō for miso ramen (Sumire / Shirakaba Sansō style), or **Daruma** for jingisukan. Soup curry (**Suage**, **Garaku**) is the other must-try.

### Sep 8 (Tue) — Otaru + Sapporo nature
- **Day:** **Otaru** (~40 min) — the canal, Sushiya-dōri sushi street, glassworks, LeTAO cheesecake, music-box museum. *(Sapporo, night 2)*
- **Back in Sapporo:** **Mt. Moiwa ropeway** for the night view, or **Maruyama Park + Hokkaidō Shrine**.
- Coffee: **Morihico** (atmospheric Sapporo roaster-café).

### Sep 9 (Wed) — Onsen-ryokan night ⭐
- Check out of Sapporo and head to **Noboribetsu** or **Lake Tōya** (~1–2h). At Noboribetsu, walk **Jigokudani "Hell Valley"** — the steaming craters that feed the baths.
- Check into an **onsen ryokan**: private/outdoor soak, yukata, and a multi-course **kaiseki dinner** as the farewell with the sister + bf. *(Onsen ryokan, night 3)*

### Sep 10 (Thu) — Fly south to Kyoto
- Onsen breakfast, short hop to New Chitose, fly CTS → Itami → **Kyoto** (~mid-afternoon). Drop bags.
- **Evening ease-in:** **Nishiki Market** (snack through it; visit **Aritsugu**, the knife maker since 1560 inside the market — a second cook's highlight) + the **Teramachi/Shinkyōgoku** arcades. Dinner in **Pontochō** by the river.

### ▶ SCHEDULE A — *Mountains* (Kyoto 4n · Osaka 2n · Takayama 3n) — **LOCKED**

**Sep 15 (Tue) — Osaka full day.** **Doguyasuji Arcade** (Namba's kitchenware street — third kitchen stop) + Kuromon in the morning. **Amerikamura** vintage, **Shinsaibashi** arcade. Coffee at **Takamura** or **Ult Coffee**. Evening **Shinsekai** kushikatsu or **Tsuruhashi** yakiniku. *(Sumiyoshi Taisha / Shitennō-ji for a calmer morning.)*

**Sep 19 (Sat) — to Tokyo** (~4.5h via Nagoya; reserve — Silver Week begins). → *Shared finale.*

## Shared finale — Sep 19–23 (identical in both)

> Silver Week is on. Tokyo handles it best, but **book restaurants and timed-entry spots ahead**, and **avoid Nikkō/Kamakura on the 21st–22nd** (mobbed) — keep day trips for the 20th or do city days.

### Sep 19 (Sat) — arrive Tokyo, regroup
- Arrive afternoon; settle into the Silver-Week hotel. Easy evening near your base: ramen, an izakaya, or world-class cocktails (**Bar High Five** Ginza, **SG Club** Shibuya, **Ben Fiddich** Shinjuku).

### Sep 20 (Sun) — the things you've been saving
- **Morning:** a strolling garden — **Rikugien** or **Hamarikyū** (tea house on the water, by Tsukiji).
- **Midday:** **Tsukiji Outer Market** seafood lunch.
- **Afternoon:** **teamLab Borderless (Azabudai)** if you skipped Planets, or **Ginza** — Itoya (stationery), Ginza Six, Uniqlo/Muji flagships, depachika halls.
- Best day for a **Kamakura** trip (Great Buddha + coast) if you'd rather leave the city — Sunday, pre-peak.

### Sep 21 (Mon, holiday) — vintage + neighborhoods (stay in town)
- **Kōenji** (raw vintage — Furugiya Sango, Kuroneko Marin) and/or **Shimokitazawa** again; record shops, cafés. Or **Nakano Broadway** (collectibles, vintage watches).
- Coffee crawl: **Glitch** (Jimbōchō) → **Onibus** (Nakameguro).

### Sep 22 (Tue, holiday) — last full day
- **Shopping sweep:** Kappabashi round 2 for knives/ceramics, Akihabara for electronics, Don Quijote / Loft / Hands for the catch-all. Or a **DisneySea** day (go early, book ahead).
- Farewell dinner: sushi omakase, kaiseki, or a great yakitori counter — reserve now.

### Sep 23 (Wed, holiday) — fly home
- Holiday traffic on airport trains — leave early. Last konbini snacks for the flight.
